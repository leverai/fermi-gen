Fix the following issue:

## Issue
- Clutter when results are revealed in the answer_accuracy_scale widget in Party mode when many players are in the game.

## Background
- Party mode is a multiplayer mode that used to be the only mode for the game.
  - It's the game service in the backend
- Players input their answers via the @answer_accuracy_scale.dart widget by sliding an indicator that has a text box showing the slider value.
- When all players submit their answer, backend computes scores and triggers a reveal event in the frontend where the scores/answers are revealed.
- For the answer accuracy scale widget, many things happen in reveal time. One of those things is indicators+text-boxes appear for each other player in the game indicating their answers in the player's unit. This helps the player visually determine how their answer compares to everyone else. Additionally, and indicator+text-box appears for the correct answer (in user's unit as well).
- Players indicators'  text boxes are placed above the indicator/scale while the correct answer's indicator is placed below the indicator/scale to prevent it from being obscured by other text boxes.
- The player can copy a question's text after reveal event by hold-pressing the @game_card.dart (where the scale widget lives).

## Problem
- players-scores' Indicators can overlap and obscure each other.
- The text boxes may implement a feature where pressing the indicator toggles the visibility of its text box, but it's not working. I think it would also be cumbersone to make working because A. pressing the thin indicator like is hard, and B. the press gesture is being recognized on the game card level (the hold-press to copy).
- Another un-related issue is that it's currently hard for the player to know the score rank for "this" question. The players row widget orders players according to their running scores, but there's no easy way to get the ranks for a single question at its reveal event.

## Proposed Solution
- Move the players answers text boxes into a carousel.
  - No text boxes for other players' answers.

## Additional Requirements
1. Preserve the formatting and style of those text boxes.
2. Place the carousel 16px below the @slider_text_mirror.dart
