## Bigger Picture
I am in the process of re-desigining the value proposition of the game. It currently revolves around this Party mode, which I don't think is the best use of the questions. As such, my bigger plan is to make this mode strictly private (no public games), and develop the DQ mode, and other modes, such as "The Guess is Right" mode (like the price is right), and a "Survival" mode where you are matched with a different player each question and you play until you loose. Additional thoughts:
- The DQ mode and Party mode need to use two distinct sets of questions from ther `fermi` table. This guarantees that the daily question has not been seen by any player before in other modes.
  - This requires making the `fermi` table hold DQ and non-DQ questions. In that case, we must modify the `get_unseen_random_questions` function (@fermi_repository.py#L123-206 )  to only work with non-DQ questions.  
- The DQ answers should not be stored in the answer events table @answer_repository.py- they should instead use a separate table dedicated for daily questions. DQ should also NOT add daily questions to user history  @user_history_repository.py mechanism.  
- The DQ time window should be from 8 am to 8 pm US central time. The user can take the DQ within this window. At the end of the question day (8 pm central time - with like 20 seconds grace time), no more submissions are allowed, and the results are computed and shown. Let's call the deadline of taking the DQ the Question Deadline (QD).
- The DQ does not show unless the player requests it. Once they do, they are only given X seconds before their answer is auto-submitted. Try 30 seconds for a start. Let's call this deadline the Answer Deadline (AD).
- The AD can also be reached in less than 30 seconds if the QD is reached. Meaning, if I start the question 10 seconds before the QD and don't submit within that time, my answer should be auto-submitted when QD is reached.
- Players should see a countdown for QD and AD.
- Players should be scored according to their answer's accuracy - can use @scoring.py.
- In th DQ mode the backend should own the timing such that all players are synchronized. Be mindful to not over-read from firestore. Timings don't have to be too accurate.
- When QD is reached, the backend shows the correct answer and the player's rank (e.g. 12/230)
